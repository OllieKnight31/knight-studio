Ainable Studio: how to install
==============================

Needs: any Mac running macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
To check: Apple menu → About This Mac.

1. Drag "Ainable Studio" into your Applications folder.

2. Open it. The first time, macOS will say it cannot verify the app and will not
   open it. That is expected for an app from outside the App Store:

     - Click "Done" on that message.
     - Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down to the line about Ainable Studio and click "Open Anyway".
     - Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

   You only do this once.

3. Ainable Studio opens on a system check. Allow the permissions it lists:

     - Screen & System Audio Recording: required to record.
     - Accessibility: required for automatic zoom (it tracks your clicks).
     - Camera and Microphone: only if you want to appear on camera / be heard.

   macOS asks you to quit and reopen the app after some of these. Do that, then
   press "Check again".

4. Record. Everything stays on your Mac unless you connect Google Drive in
   Settings.

Updating: replace the app in Applications with the new one. macOS treats an
update as a new app, so it will ask for the permissions in step 3 again.

Captions download a speech model (about 150 MB) the first time you use them.
After that they work offline. Your audio is never uploaded.
